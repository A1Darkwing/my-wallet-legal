Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Summary

  • We only collect what the app needs to work: your email, your name and the bookkeeping data you enter.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data with third parties.
  • You can export your data to CSV and permanently delete your account at any time, right in the app.

1. Data we collect

  • Account information: the email address and display name provided by Google or Apple when you sign in. If you choose “Hide My Email” with Sign in with Apple, we only receive the relay address generated by Apple.
  • Financial data you enter: accounts, income and expense categories, journal entries, budgets, loans, recurring transactions and notes. This is data you record yourself; the app does not connect to banks and cannot access your real bank accounts.
  • On-device preferences: your theme and whether Face ID lock is enabled are stored locally on your device and never sent to our servers.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or biometric data.

2. Face ID / Touch ID

The app lock uses iOS biometric authentication. Authentication happens entirely on your device; the app only receives a “success / failure” result and never accesses or stores your face or fingerprint data.

3. How we use your data

Your data is used only to provide the app's features: signing in, storing and syncing your books, calculating balances and producing reports. We do not use your data for advertising, behavioral analytics or model training.

4. Storage and security

  • Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure, with servers in Singapore.
  • All connections between the app and the server are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • The database enforces Row Level Security: each user can only read and write their own data.

5. Third parties

We only use the providers needed to run the service:

  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication.
  • Google and Apple — sign-in. Use of these services is subject to the privacy policies of Google and Apple.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, unless required by law.

6. Your rights

  • Access and export: Settings → Export journal (CSV).
  • Correction: every account and entry can be edited in the app.
  • Account deletion: Settings → Delete Account. Your sign-in account and all bookkeeping data are permanently deleted from our servers immediately and cannot be recovered.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. For significant changes, we will also notify you in the app.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions, please see the Support & Contact page.
