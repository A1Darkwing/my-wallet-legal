Frequently asked questions

Does the app connect to my bank?
No. All figures are entered by you; the app does not hold or move real money.

How do I back up my data?
Your data is stored on the server and linked to your account. You can also export a copy at Settings → Export journal (CSV).

What if I switch phones?
Sign in again with the same Google/Apple account and your data will sync automatically.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete Account. All your data is permanently deleted immediately.

I can't unlock with Face ID.
You can unlock with your device passcode. You can also turn the lock off in Settings → Security.

How do I change the app language?
Settings → Language opens the iOS settings for the app, where you can pick Tiếng Việt or English.
